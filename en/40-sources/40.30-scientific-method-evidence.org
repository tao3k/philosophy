This note synthesizes shared constraints from modern scientific method concerning observable evidence, counterexamples, measurement error, reproducibility, and uncertainty.

[[file:../README.org][English navigation]] · [[file:../30-reflections/30.50-unknown-as-first-class.org][corresponding reflection]]

* Context

Schools of philosophy of science disagree. This is a Tao3k methodological synthesis, not a claim that one school is the sole authority.

* Interpretation Boundaries

Probabilities, confidence intervals, and model fit do not automatically create causal facts; absence of a counterexample is not a global proof.

* Influence

It informs reflections on unknown as a first-class state and on the simulation/prediction boundary.

* Interpretation Chain

Historical source → Tao3k modern interpretation → [[file:../30-reflections/30.50-unknown-as-first-class.org][corresponding reflection]] → possible thematic Charter.
