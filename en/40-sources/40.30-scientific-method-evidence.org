This note synthesizes constraints from modern scientific methods concerning observable evidence, counterexamples, measurement error, reproducibility, and uncertainty. Concrete anchors include [[https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297][NIST Technical Note 1297]], which requires measurement results to be reported with uncertainty and the method used to evaluate it. Popperian empirical risk is handled in a [[file:40.70-popper-falsifiability.org][separate source note]]. The synthesis does not collapse them into one school.

[[file:../README.org][English navigation]] · [[file:../30-reflections/30.50-unknown-as-first-class.org][corresponding reflection]]

* Context

Scientific method is not one algorithm. Measurement traditions address the measurand, calibration, systematic effects, and uncertainty budgets; statistical inference addresses sampling, models, and updating; falsifiability asks whether an empirical claim risks failure; replication and reproduction examine dependence on operators, instruments, and environments. These traditions complement but do not substitute for one another.

NIST TN 1297 emphasizes that the usefulness of a measurement depends on a quality statement of uncertainty and asks reports to expose components and evaluation methods. This is a discipline for measurement reporting, not a complete taxonomy of epistemic unknown. Tao3k's extension from “report a result with its boundary” to Agent knowledge objects is an explicit modernization.

* Interpretation Boundaries

Probabilities, confidence intervals, and model fit do not automatically create causal facts. Absence of a counterexample is not global proof, and reproducibility does not ensure transfer to a new population. Measurement uncertainty concerns knowledge of a quantity under a declared method; it does not automatically include model error, conceptual choice, value conflict, or unknown common causes.

Bayesian updating likewise depends on a likelihood, prior, and model space. A high posterior does not prove that the correct explanation was available, nor can data replace normative judgment. Tao3k does not compress every dispute into one confidence score under the name of scientific method.
