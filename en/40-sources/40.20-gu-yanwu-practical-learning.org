* Context

These entries discuss the purpose, substance, and quantity of writing: more writing does not itself guarantee value. Their moral and political commitments are not a neutral modern utility formula. The following AI questions begin here without treating two short entries as a complete account of Gu Yanwu's thought.

Practical learning resists discourse detached from facts, institutions, and real problems, but it is not short-term utilitarianism or single-objective optimization. Modern technical usefulness must keep asking who benefits, who bears cost, which problem actually improved, which dependencies were created, and how long-term options changed.

This returns benchmarks, throughput, and automation ratios to the status of evidence. They may establish a capability change but cannot independently establish increased public value. The object of statecraft is consequence in lived and institutional reality, not the metric itself.

In our modernization, “I did it for your good” likewise cannot establish help. Someone may understand what matters while lacking conditions for action; the benefit-harm inquiry should then examine missing opportunities, support and institutions rather than blame weak will. Knowledge should enter practice, while practice remains answerable to facts and consequences.

* Interpretation Boundaries

It cannot endorse every automation labelled useful or reduce “the public” to aggregate majority utility. Irreversible harm borne by minorities, diminished agency, maintenance debt, and future exit costs all belong to the benefit-harm account. Gu Yanwu did not supply a modern AI scorecard; the present question framework remains this repository's responsibility.

Nor does this note reduce practical learning to deployment, transaction or task completion. Where structural support is absent, identifying and attempting to change conditions can itself be a practical result; absence of immediate execution does not mean absence of a real problem.

* Influence

Within [[file:../30-reflections/30.300-dialogues-across-time.org][Conversations across time]], this entry also questions our own writing: do additional essays develop a problem or repeat a position? It must not become a demand that all rest, appreciation, and private writing prove a public output.

It enters [[file:../30-reflections/30.20-practical-learning-human-centered-ai.org][Practical Learning and Human-Centered AI]], moving evaluation from performance increase to benefit, burden, dependency, reversibility, and long-term optionality while preventing a Charter from reducing human value to a metric.
