This note interprets practical learning as requiring knowledge to answer to concrete harms, benefits, and public consequences rather than slogans. Edition and passage-level sourcing remain pending.

[[file:../README.org][English navigation]] · [[file:../30-reflections/30.20-practical-learning-human-centered-ai.org][corresponding reflection]]

* Context

Practical learning resists discourse detached from facts and real problems, but it is not short-term utilitarianism or single-objective optimization.

* Interpretation Boundaries

It cannot endorse every automation labelled useful; beneficiaries, burden bearers, and evidential sufficiency remain review questions.

* Influence

It enters the reflection on practical learning and human-centered AI, constraining any Charter from reducing human value to a metric.

* Interpretation Chain

Historical source → Tao3k modern interpretation → [[file:../30-reflections/30.20-practical-learning-human-centered-ai.org][corresponding reflection]] → possible thematic Charter. The direction preserves the distinction between source, interpretation, and norm; it must not be reversed into a claim that the source proved the current architecture.
