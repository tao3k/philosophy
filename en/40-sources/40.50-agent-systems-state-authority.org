This note synthesizes the design tendency across several agent-system projects to keep state,
proposal, decision, authorization, execution, and outcome receipts distinct. It does not claim a
time-bound engineering observation. Promotion to =ENGINEERING_EVIDENCE= requires a separate record
with repository, revision, RFC/path, and observation date.

[[file:../README.org][English navigation]] · [[file:../30-reflections/30.60-action-requires-authority.org][corresponding reflection]]

* Context

Tool availability is not authority to act, model output is not a decision, and invocation success is not sufficient reconciliation of real-world outcomes.

* Interpretation Boundaries

Current Tao3k implementations are replaceable realizations and cannot define permanent philosophical norms in reverse.

* Influence

It enters the action/authority reflection and provides engineering counterexamples for the thematic Charter.

* Interpretation Chain

Engineering-practice synthesis → Tao3k modern interpretation → [[file:../30-reflections/30.60-action-requires-authority.org][corresponding reflection]] → possible thematic Charter. The direction preserves the distinction between synthesis, interpretation, and norm; it must not be reversed into a claim that the synthesis proved the current architecture.
