This note records current engineering evidence for keeping state, proposal, decision, authorization,
execution, and outcome receipts distinct in agent systems. Until =SOURCE_REVISIONS= and =SOURCE_PATHS=
are replaced by concrete receipts, it remains a synthesis awaiting downstream evidence.

[[file:../README.org][English navigation]] · [[file:../30-reflections/30.60-action-requires-authority.org][corresponding reflection]]

* Context

Tool availability is not authority to act, model output is not a decision, and invocation success is not sufficient reconciliation of real-world outcomes.

* Interpretation Boundaries

Current Tao3k implementations are replaceable realizations and cannot define permanent philosophical norms in reverse.

* Influence

It enters the action/authority reflection and provides engineering counterexamples for the thematic Charter.

* Interpretation Chain

Historical source → Tao3k modern interpretation → [[file:../30-reflections/30.60-action-requires-authority.org][corresponding reflection]] → possible thematic Charter. The direction preserves the distinction between source, interpretation, and norm; it must not be reversed into a claim that the source proved the current architecture.
