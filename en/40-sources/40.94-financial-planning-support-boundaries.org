This note combines the [[https://www.moneyguidepro.com/ifa/home/faqs][MoneyGuide FAQ]], [[https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/policy-statements/ps25-22-consumer-pensions-investment-decisions-rules-targeted-support][FCA PS25/22]], and [[https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/innovation/innovation-pathways/automated-advice-rules-guidance][FCA automated advice rules and guidance]] to compare market capability, UK targeted support, and automated financial activities. The automated-advice page was updated on 24 July 2026 and observed on 21 September 2026.
* Context
MoneyGuide already offers goal-based planning, What If scenarios, stress tests, sensitivity, and Monte Carlo, so multiple-future simulation is not a Tao3k innovation. FCA targeted support went live on 6 April 2026, allowing authorized firms to give suggestions designed for groups sharing characteristics as a regulated activity distinct from full personalized advice.
* Interpretation Boundaries
Product documentation does not prove correctness or alignment with Tao3k. UK categories are not global law. Legal boundaries among education, simulation, support, guidance, recommendation, management, and execution require jurisdiction-specific analysis.

The FCA automated-advice page covers pensions, insurance, mortgages, and debt counselling as well as investments; it is neither formal Handbook guidance nor exhaustive. Avoiding stock recommendations or using a Zhisheng simulation label therefore establishes no general regulatory exemption. Examine the actual activity, not its product name.
* Influence
The market comparison locates M4's difference in decision legitimacy rather than scenario features. The regulatory comparison requires product and authority models to distinguish support layers rather than hide them in a disclaimer.
* Interpretation Chain
Existing market capability plus UK support and automated-activity boundaries → distinguish support content from actual behavior → [[file:../30-reflections/30.92-stewardship-not-sovereignty.org][Stewardship]] permissions composed by object, action, disclosure, and time, not an automatic autonomy ladder. The philosophical case for reasoned recommendations does not itself determine legal permission.
