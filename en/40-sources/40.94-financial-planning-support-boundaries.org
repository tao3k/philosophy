This note synthesizes the [[https://www.moneyguidepro.com/ifa/home/faqs][MoneyGuide FAQ]] as a market-capability comparison and [[https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/policy-statements/ps25-22-consumer-pensions-investment-decisions-rules-targeted-support][FCA PS25/22]] as a UK-specific regulatory regime.
* Context
MoneyGuide already offers goal-based planning, What If scenarios, stress tests, sensitivity, and Monte Carlo, so multiple-future simulation is not a Tao3k innovation. FCA targeted support went live on 6 April 2026, allowing authorized firms to give suggestions designed for groups sharing characteristics as a regulated activity distinct from full personalized advice.
* Interpretation Boundaries
Product documentation does not prove correctness or alignment with Tao3k. UK categories are not global law. Legal boundaries among education, simulation, support, guidance, recommendation, management, and execution require jurisdiction-specific analysis.
* Influence
The market comparison locates M4's difference in decision legitimacy rather than scenario features. The regulatory comparison requires product and authority models to distinguish support layers rather than hide them in a disclaimer.
* Interpretation Chain
Existing market capability plus UK targeted-support classification → visible support layers → Observe-to-Act authority gradient in [[file:../30-reflections/30.92-stewardship-not-sovereignty.org][Stewardship]].
