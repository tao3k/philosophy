This note places the unity of knowledge and action in the Ming intellectual and moral-practice context. No critical edition is pinned yet, so it is an interpretive entry rather than evidence for verbatim quotation.

[[file:../README.org][English navigation]] · [[file:../30-reflections/30.10-knowledge-action-in-agent-age.org][corresponding reflection]]

* Context

Knowledge is not an arbitrary proposition cache, and action is not a tool call. The historical concern is that moral understanding, intention, and practice cannot be separated for convenience.

* Interpretation Boundaries

It may inspire agent knowledge–action closure, but it does not prove any protocol, runtime, or authority model correct.

* Influence

It enters the reflection on knowledge and action in the agent age and may be promoted only after counterexamples, authority boundaries, and engineering consequences are explicit.
