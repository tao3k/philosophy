* Source
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: source-method-en
:END:

The source layer admits classical Chinese thought, modern philosophy of
science, formal methods, and computer-systems thought into shared inquiry. See
the [[file:../README.org][English root]] for repository navigation.

| SOURCE_KIND | Meaning | Minimum locator |
|-------------+---------+-----------------|
| PRIMARY | First-hand material from an author, institution, or system | Edition, passage, or stable locator |
| SECONDARY | Scholarly interpretation of primary material | Author, publication, edition, and argumentative scope |
| SYNTHESIS | Explicit Tao3k synthesis across sources | Constituent sources, interpretive distance, and counterexample boundary |
| ENGINEERING_EVIDENCE | Time-bound observation of current engineering state | Repository, revision, RFC/path, and observation date |

* Context

Concepts from Wang Yangming, Gu Yanwu, or modern thinkers retain their
historical context. They are not a vocabulary to map directly onto protocol fields.

* Interpretation Boundaries

The unity of knowledge and action can inspire a closed knowledge-action loop,
but does not prove one agent architecture correct. Practical learning can
constrain purpose without reducing human value to utility maximization.

* Influence

A source enters Reflection first. It may reach Charter only after review of
counterexamples, boundaries, and engineering consequences. Citation alone does
not confer normative authority.

* Interpretation Chain

Source identity and historical context are recorded first; the interpretation
declares whether it is direct, analogical, or modernized; Reflection tests its
counterexamples and boundaries; only a governance-reviewed conclusion may enter
Charter, after which an Engineering Map separates requirements from realization.

=edition-pending= and =evidence-pending= are disclosed gaps; they must not be presented as completed
source grounding.
