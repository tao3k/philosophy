Grounding: the abstract and definitions of performative risk and stability in the [[https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/perdomo20a/perdomo20a.pdf][paper]].
* Context
Predictions used in decisions can change the outcomes and distributions they predict. The paper formalizes this feedback and studies repeated training's convergence under specified conditions.
* Interpretation Boundaries
This does not imply all prediction is impossible, all advice manipulates, or every life situation satisfies the assumptions. Statistical stability does not establish a worthwhile life.
* Influence
Our proposed application asks whether displaying a life trajectory changes attention, investment and self-understanding. If advice makes alternatives less visible, subsequent predictive accuracy may be insufficient to assess its value.

This is our inquiry into simulation displays and judgment, not a claimed measurement result. We particularly ask who can refuse such shaping and how unchosen paths remain discussable.
