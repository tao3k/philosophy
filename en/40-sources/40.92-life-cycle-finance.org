This note uses the Boston Fed paper [[https://www.bostonfed.org/publications/public-policy-discussion-paper/2007/the-theory-of-life-cycle-saving-and-investing.aspx][The Theory of Life-Cycle Saving and Investing]].
* Context
The paper gives a nonmathematical life-cycle treatment of retirement, education, insurance, and allocation, presenting financial assets as vehicles for moving resources across times and possible outcomes.
* Interpretation Boundaries
The model cannot choose human ends or let consumption smoothing, lifetime wealth, or standard preferences replace care, meaning, and rights. It is an analytical framework, not a personal instruction.
* Influence
It supports resource trajectories across time, bounded by [[file:../30-reflections/30.93-intertemporal-agency.org][Intertemporal Agency]] so future security and present life cannot silently consume one another.
* Interpretation Chain
Life-cycle finance → allocation across time/outcomes → financial trajectories → Tao3k's normative extension to future selves, reversibility, and optionality.
