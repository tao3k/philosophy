This note synthesizes four groups of web materials: Apple's [[https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/design-principles][design principles]],
[[https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/disclosure-controls][disclosure controls]] and [[https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility][accessibility guidance]], together with the W3C WAI pattern for
[[https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/patterns/o5p01-minimal-interruptions/][limiting interruptions]] and its [[https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG20/Understanding/interruptions][explanation of controllable interruptions]]. The pages were observed on
2026-09-21.
* Context
These are interface and accessibility guidelines, not philosophical findings about a good life. Apple places purpose,
agency, responsibility and simplicity among its design considerations while warning that simplicity is not mere
minimalism. Disclosure controls show detail when relevant while keeping likely or primary actions visible. W3C's
materials address attention, memory and cognitive barriers by asking that people be able to postpone, suppress or
control non-emergency interruptions, reminders and content changes.

Together they face a practical tension. Showing every detail at once creates burden, while hiding information and
recovery paths needed for a decision weakens agency. Progressive disclosure is therefore not a rule to show less. It
relates the timing and level of information to its relevance and consequence.
* Interpretation Boundaries
These guidelines do not prove that a product improves life, respects refusal or avoids shifting labor. “Agency” in
design language is not automatically the same as human authorship in this repository. An interface may conform
visually while defaults, commercial ranking, hidden exit and excessive memory control outcomes.

The emergency exception in W3C guidance must not be stretched until conversion, engagement or ordinary
recommendation counts as urgent. Apple's progressive disclosure cannot decide which costs, risks and irreversible
effects must appear first. These living pages may change; this note records the observed wording and scope.
* Influence
The materials support a limited engineering judgment: room can take the form of controllable interruption, detail on
demand, clear primary action, easy recovery and data collection bounded by the present purpose. They also show why
convergence does not erase complexity. It presents what the current decision needs first and lets a person know what
else exists, why it is folded away and how to inspect it.

[[file:../30-reflections/30.170-when-less-use-is-good.org][“Using It Less May Be a Good Outcome”]] therefore treats user space as room for attention, time, judgment,
expression, relation and change rather than blank pixels. [[file:../30-reflections/ai-object-shift/30.180-beyond-gui-user-space.org][“What Must Remain Visible Beyond GUI”]] carries the
question into agentic AI: a model may spare step-by-step operation while goals, authority, consequences, evidence and
takeover remain visible.
* Interpretation Chain
Apple on purpose, agency, recovery, necessary data and disclosure + W3C on user control of non-emergency interruption
→ limited contemporary methods for being spare without abandoning responsibility
→ Tao3k's modern interpretation of room, convergence and user space. The final step is this repository's argument,
not a conclusion stated by the sources.
