This note uses the CFPB page [[https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/financial-well-being/about/][Why financial well-being?]].
* Context
CFPB frames financial well-being as security and freedom of choice: day-to-day control, capacity to absorb shock, progress toward goals, and freedom to enjoy life. It explicitly says income, net worth, and credit score alone cannot describe individual well-being.
* Interpretation Boundaries
The framework is not investment advice, a utility function, or a universal life ranking, and it does not make the CFPB questionnaire an Agent objective. Regulatory implications remain jurisdiction-specific.
* Influence
It grounds [[file:../30-reflections/30.91-financial-life-simulation.org][Financial Life Simulation]]: security and choice coexist, and financial outcome cannot collapse into net worth.
* Interpretation Chain
CFPB consumer research → financial security and freedom → Tao3k resilience, goal trajectory, and optionality. The choice frontier is a further synthesis here.
