This note synthesizes four groups of materials: the UI-JEPA [[https://doi.org/10.1145/3699682.3728327][ACM UMAP 2025 record]] and
[[https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04081][preprint]]; Meta AI Research's [[https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-spark-meta-model-api][Muse Spark 1.1 introduction]]; the Meta Model API
[[https://dev.meta.ai/docs/computer-use][computer-use documentation]]; and Meta's publications on [[https://research.meta.ai/blog/security-and-safety-for-ai-agents-our-approach-with-muse][Muse safety design]] and its
[[https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/][product form]]. The paper appeared in 2025; the Meta pages were observed on 2026-09-21.
* Context
UI-JEPA studies how to summarize user intent from a sequence of interface actions. A video encoder observes the
process and a language model produces a textual account of likely intent. This is a problem of inferring what an
action sequence appears to pursue, not of deciding how far a system is authorized to act for the person now.

The official Muse Spark 1.1 introduction places the model in cross-application, tool-use and computer-use tasks and
describes choices among scripts, clicks and batched actions. Meta's computer-use documentation describes an
observe-act loop: the model proposes an action from a screenshot, the host application executes it and returns a new
screenshot. The host owns the environment, execution, validation, logging and recovery.

Muse product and safety materials also describe confirmation before sending email or purchasing, visible browser
activity, user takeover, connected-app control and an audit trail. These vendor descriptions offer a concrete object
for discussion. They are not independent evidence of outcomes.
* Interpretation Boundaries
An UI-JEPA intent description may be wrong and may summarize only a past sequence. It cannot establish a person's
durable purpose, consent, legal authority or continuing willingness. Accuracy or small-model efficiency does not turn
intent inference into authorization.

The Muse materials do not prove reliability, safety or greater freedom in every setting. A publisher's account of its
system cannot replace external evaluation. The ability to use a script instead of clicks does not make GUI unnecessary,
nor does it make runtime state, effects or errors safe to hide. A screenshot supports only the visible state at that
moment; it cannot alone prove background data, external recipients, shared rights or actual completion.

These materials may also change with the products. This note records public boundaries at observation time rather
than treating one roadmap as an inevitable future.
* Influence
The materials move interaction beyond screen arrangement toward who executes, who understands, who authorizes and
who can stop. A model's execution entrance may be a tool, script or interface. A person's entrance must preserve the
goal, material assumptions, authority scope, objects about to change, failure, evidence, reversal and takeover. The two
entrances need not look alike, but neither can replace the other.

They also support a crucial distinction. Intent inference can reduce repeated explanation but cannot grant authority.
An action trace can support accountability but cannot replace a person's decision before serious effects. Confirmation
can protect a person, but it must converge by risk so repeated dialogs do not hand responsibility back to the person.
* Interpretation Chain
UI-JEPA infers intent from action sequences + Muse Spark executes through scripts, tools and interfaces + Meta's host
contract leaves validation, logging, recovery and safety checks to the application + product materials claim approval,
visible activity, takeover and audit
→ the model's execution interface begins to separate from the person's understanding and authority interface
→ [[file:../30-reflections/ai-object-shift/30.180-beyond-gui-user-space.org][“What Must Remain Visible Beyond GUI”]] argues that operation may disappear while boundaries of responsibility
must not. The last step is Tao3k's philosophical argument, not a conclusion jointly endorsed by the sources.
