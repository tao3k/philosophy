* Principle
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: engineering-principle-en
:END:

The [[file:../10-charter/10.00-tao3k-charter.org][Tao3k Charter]] requires knowledge, judgment, assurance, authority,
execution, and consequences to remain traceable and non-collapsed.

* Normative Requirements

| Stage | Engineering property | Must not substitute | Typical owner |
|-------+---------------+---------------------+---------------|
| Know | source identity, generation, provenance | model confidence | ASP / MRR |
| Discern | dependency, causality, unknown frontier | plausible completion | MRR |
| Assure | proof, verification, evidence sufficiency | linguistic fluency | Aitia / POO Flow verifier handoff |
| Act | decision, policy, authority, intervention | tool availability | ASP / Marlin |
| Reconcile | receipt, observation, invalidation | invocation return code | POO Flow / MRR |

* Review Questions

A design review asks what is known, what remains unknown, what relations support
the judgment, what assurance is required, who has authority, how the world will
change, how the outcome returns as knowledge, and what happens when it is ambiguous.
