principles of the Tao3k ecosystem. It produces no runtime and does not replace
project RFCs. See the [[file:../cn/README.org][Chinese root]] for its semantic counterpart.

justified; actions must be authorized; consequences must return as knowledge.

* AI Interaction-Object Shift Topic

This topic does not extend traditional GUI guidance directly into the agent era as an answer. It distinguishes three
objects that are already different: a person directly operating software; a person and AI forming an acting relation;
and an agent becoming a service's direct caller while people and other affected parties remain at purpose, authority,
consequence and appeal. Inherited standards are historical baselines, not automatic standards for the new object.

Topic documents compose the ordinary Reflection contract with
=philosophy.ai-object-reflection.en.v1=. The contract requires each document to state the traditional object and its
applicability boundary, the object shift, a present judgment, scenes containing both supporting and opposing reasons,
and the boundary between Principle and Governance. It constrains completeness without prematurely promoting a
provisional judgment into a principle.
