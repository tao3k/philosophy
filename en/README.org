Tao3k Philosophy maintains the shared thought, concepts, and normative
principles of the Tao3k ecosystem. It produces no runtime and does not replace
project RFCs. See the [[file:../cn/README.org][Chinese root]] for its semantic counterpart.

#+begin_quote
Knowledge must be grounded; reasoning must preserve evidence; decisions must be
justified; actions must be authorized; consequences must return as knowledge.
#+end_quote

* Enter Through Questions About Life
Begin with [[file:30-reflections/30.96-living-with-ai.org][Facing Life with AI: Purposes, Possibilities, and Judgment]]. Life is not a fixed objective function, simulation is not a verdict of destiny, and AI's value extends beyond safer task execution. Zhisheng supplies a practical entry; education, care, changing abilities, relocation, creation and retirement also belong here.

These paths connect reflections still requiring scrutiny, not claims all promoted to Charter. Actual improvement needs experience from participants and affected people; complete documents and consistent logic cannot substitute for it.
