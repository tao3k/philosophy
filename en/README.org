Tao3k Philosophy maintains the shared thought, concepts, and normative
principles of the Tao3k ecosystem. It produces no runtime and does not replace
project RFCs. See the [[file:../cn/README.org][Chinese root]] for its semantic counterpart.

Knowledge must be grounded; reasoning must preserve evidence; decisions must be
justified; actions must be authorized; consequences must return as knowledge.
