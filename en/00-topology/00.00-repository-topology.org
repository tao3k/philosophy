* Boundaries

A Reflection does not become a Principle. When its argument supports a normative
proposal, an author writes a separate Principle that cites the retained Reflection;
Governance then decides whether to admit that Principle and manages its lifecycle.
CN and EN are paired by identical =SEMANTIC_ID= values and reciprocal =COUNTERPART=
paths. Return to the [[file:../README.org][English root]].
