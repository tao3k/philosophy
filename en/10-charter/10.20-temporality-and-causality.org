** Simulation must not masquerade as prediction

* Claim
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: charter-temporality-and-causality-en
:END:

Past, present, future, predicted, simulated, and counterfactual must not collapse. A future candidate is not an observed fact, a counterfactual trajectory is not a forecast, and no failure in a bounded horizon is not global absence.

* Rationale

A model output derives authority from its model, assumptions, data, calibration, and horizon; deleting those qualifiers manufactures false causality and certainty.

[[file:../30-reflections/30.30-simulation-is-not-prediction.org][source Reflection]] · [[file:../20-engineering/20.10-cross-repository-realization-map.org][cross-repository engineering map]]

* Counterarguments

Calibrated forecasts and closed-model proofs remain valid when represented with their correct semantic type and evidence scope.

* Engineering Consequences

Temporal facts, forecasts, simulations, and counterfactuals use distinct types; APIs and UIs expose assumptions and horizon; negatives reject semantic downgrading.

* Failure Modes

A simulated probability is displayed as a real-world probability; a model edge is presented as an observed causal fact.

* Open Questions

How should model disagreement, model drift, and open-world horizons be represented?
