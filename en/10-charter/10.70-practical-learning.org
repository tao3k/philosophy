* Claim
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: charter-practical-learning-en
:END:

A technical claim connects to real human problems, benefits, costs, and observable effects; abstract optimization, architectural elegance, or a benchmark cannot alone prove value.

* Rationale

Practical learning is modernized here as traceable problem → intervention → outcome → learning, not as historical authority endorsing a product.

[[file:../30-reflections/30.20-practical-learning-human-centered-ai.org][source Reflection]] · [[file:../20-engineering/20.10-cross-repository-realization-map.org][cross-repository engineering map]]

* Counterarguments

Basic research and platform engineering may lack immediate outcomes; long-term value must allow exploration and contributions not immediately measurable.

* Engineering Consequences

RFCs state problem owners, intended effects, negative externalities, and observation plans; post-run outcomes return to Reflection.

* Failure Modes

Usage substitutes for welfare; benefits are reported without costs; historical citation is treated as normative proof.

* Open Questions

How should non-quantifiable value be handled? How can stakeholder conflicts remain visible rather than averaged away?
