* Claim
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: charter-claim-en
:END:

Knowledge and action in an AI system form a loop constrained by evidence,
assurance, and authority: =know → discern → assure → act → reconcile → know=.

* Rationale

Parametric recall is not auditable knowledge, confidence is not truth, and tool
success is not task completion. The world has history, causality, and
irreversible consequences; an agent is not merely =prompt → response=.

* Counterarguments

The framework may burden low-risk work, formal assurance cannot exhaust value
judgment, and human authorization can itself be wrong. These objections require
graded evidence and revocable authority rather than collapsed boundaries.

* Engineering Consequences

- =proposal != decision != authority != execution=.
- Irreversibility raises the evidence threshold and narrows authority.
- A receipt is an input to reconciliation and knowledge revision, not decoration.

* Failure Modes

Confidence presented as truth, absence projected as false, an objective
function presented as human obligation, and invocation success presented as
verified world change all violate this Charter.

* Open Questions

How should assurance scale by risk? How can urgent automation retain revocable
human agency? When observations conflict, when may prior knowledge remain usable?
