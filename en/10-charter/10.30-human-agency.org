* Claim
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: charter-human-agency-en
:END:

A high-impact system identifies who defines the goal, who is affected, and how people consent, override, refuse, and contest. Optimization results are not human value or consent.

* Rationale

No single proxy fully represents human value; agency requires exit, correction, appeal, and the ability to redefine goals.

[[file:../40-sources/40.97-transformative-experience-evaluability.org][source note]] · [[file:../30-reflections/30.40-optimization-is-not-human-value.org][retained Reflection]] · [[file:../20-engineering/20.10-cross-repository-realization-map.org][cross-repository engineering map]]

* Counterarguments

Explicit delegation and low-risk automation can reduce per-action confirmation, but scope, duration, and revocation remain explicit.

* Engineering Consequences

Policy and UI expose goal owner, affected parties, consent state, overrides, and challenge receipts.

* Failure Modes

Dark patterns make exit impractical; proxy objectives are treated as welfare; affected people lack a challenge path.

* Open Questions

Who decides when group goals conflict? When must long-lived delegation be reconfirmed?
