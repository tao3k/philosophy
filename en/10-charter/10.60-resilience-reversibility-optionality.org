* Claim
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: charter-resilience-reversibility-optionality-en
:END:

Decisions treat reversibility, resilience, and future optionality as first-class consequences. Short-term local optima must not silently create irrecoverable dependency or a single point of failure.

* Rationale

Unknowns and environmental change cannot be eliminated; recovery paths, alternatives, and exit rights reduce the long-term cost of mistaken judgment.

[[file:../40-sources/40.97-transformative-experience-evaluability.org][source note]] · [[file:../30-reflections/30.40-optimization-is-not-human-value.org][retained Reflection]] · [[file:../20-engineering/20.10-cross-repository-realization-map.org][cross-repository engineering map]]

* Counterarguments

Some irreversible commitments are necessary; redundancy and options have real costs; prolonged indecision also loses opportunities.

* Engineering Consequences

High-impact changes declare rollback or compensation, blast radius, fallback, lock-in, and recovery-drill evidence.

* Failure Modes

Rollback exists only in documentation; backups are never restored; local benchmarks hide system fragility.

* Open Questions

How should option cost be assessed? When should an irreversible commitment be accepted?
