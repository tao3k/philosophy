* Claim
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: charter-proportional-assurance-en
:END:

Required assurance increases with irreversibility, impact radius, uncertainty, and authority scope. This is a normative relation, not pretend mathematical precision.

* Rationale

Reading a webpage, editing a draft, deploying production, transferring money, trading, and medical intervention must not share one confidence threshold or execution path.

[[file:../30-reflections/30.10-knowledge-action-in-agent-age.org][source Reflection]] · [[file:../20-engineering/20.10-cross-repository-realization-map.org][cross-repository engineering map]]

* Counterarguments

Assurance cost can itself cause delay and harm; emergencies need controlled degradation; low-risk actions must not be burdened by high-risk procedure.

* Engineering Consequences

Profiles select evidence, verification, approval, rollback, and observation by risk; degradation records rationale and receipts.

* Failure Modes

One LLM confidence triggers every action; thresholds rise without independent evidence; an old profile survives risk escalation.

* Open Questions

How should the four factors compose without cosmetic quantification? Who approves emergency degradation?
