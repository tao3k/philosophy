* Question
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: reflection-action-requires-authority-en
:END:

When an executable tool exists, what additional conditions authorize an agent to change the external world?

[[file:../40-sources/40.50-agent-systems-state-authority.org][source note]] · [[file:../10-charter/10.40-action-and-authority.org][target thematic Charter]]

* Argument

A proposal describes what could be done, a decision selects whether to act, authority proves who permits it, execution changes the world, and an outcome receipt records the result. Each stage must be independently rejectable.

* Counterexamples

Policy may pre-authorize low-risk actions; emergency stops may lack per-action human approval; distributed authority may be a capability rather than a click.

* Provisional Conclusion

Action requires explicit authority scope, resources, time bounds, revocation or compensation paths, and outcome receipts.

* Promotion Criteria

Promote when positive and negative authority cases and failure modes are executable.
