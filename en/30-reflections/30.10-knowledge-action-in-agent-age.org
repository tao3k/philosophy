* Question
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: reflection-knowledge-action-in-agent-age-en
:END:

When an agent can read, reason, propose, receive authority, and invoke tools, how can knowledge and action belong to one chain of responsibility without collapsing facts, inference, judgment, authority, execution, and outcome into an unreviewable black box?

[[file:../40-sources/40.10-wang-yangming-knowledge-action.org][source note]] · [[file:../10-charter/10.40-action-and-authority.org][target thematic Charter]]

* Argument

Wang Yangming's unity of knowing and acting first addresses a separation: treating verbal assent as completed knowledge while postponing conduct as an unrelated later task. The criticism still matters for agents. A system that recites a principle but cannot let it constrain proposals, refuse unauthorized action, or revise later judgment holds a retrievable statement, not knowledge that has entered practice.

Modernization cannot mean that whatever is known must be executed. A system may know a risk but lack authority to alter the world. It may form a sound proposal but properly await human consent. It may decline to act because the action is irreversible. Non-action does not by itself disprove knowledge, and correct motivation does not legitimize unauthorized execution. The Tao3k revision is that genuine knowledge must be able to enter the action structure correctly while respecting authority, risk, human agency, and refusal.

** Knowing while presently unable to act
Modern life must recognize another separation. Someone may understand how they wish to live and be willing to bear the relevant responsibility, yet lack money, time, opportunity, rights, accessibility or necessary cooperation. Inability to act may expose missing conditions; it does not automatically establish false knowledge, insincere motivation or insufficient prompting.

AI should therefore do more than push a correct judgment toward a tool call. It should distinguish missing facts, capabilities, authority and real support; identify conditions that can be built gradually; recognize organizational or institutional responsibility; and preserve currently unrealizable aspirations as directions. Improving conditions and respecting present inability can both belong to practice rather than moralizing hardship as individual failure.

This is also a limit on historical interpretation. Wang Yangming addresses moral understanding, motivation and cultivation, not a formula for blaming people under modern deprivation or constraint. Tao3k retains the rejection of verbal assent as completed practice while refusing to infer “not genuine knowledge” directly from “not acted upon.”

Unity therefore names a relation of responsibility and revision, not the disappearance of types:

#+begin_example
source → observation → claim → reasoning → judgment
       → decision → authority → execution → outcome → new observation
#+end_example

Each object remains separately challengeable. A fact may be true while a decision is unjustified; a decision may be reasonable while the actor is unauthorized; an execution may return success while its real-world consequence fails. Layering is not a betrayal of unity. It is what prevents unity from becoming opaque domination in an AI system.

Closure also requires outcomes to alter knowledge. Action is not the terminus of knowledge but an experimental condition under which the world supplies further evidence. If an outcome is not observed, reconciled, and returned to a revisable epistemic state, execution has completed but learning has not.

* Counterexamples

Read-only inquiry need not carry production-grade authorization. Assurance should be proportional to consequence rather than imposed uniformly on every step.

An emergency stop may act before a complete explanation, but the exception still needs a trigger, an authority source, and retrospective review. Otherwise emergency becomes a permanent path around responsibility.

Some outcomes are observable only after delay, and others remain only partially observable. This does not defeat closure. It requires explicit pending, unknown, and unobservable states, and forbids recording “no failure found yet” as “success established.”

Finally, ethical motivation and engineering reliability cannot substitute for one another. An honest but incapable agent can still cause harm; an accurate but unauthorized agent still may not act. Unity does not remove the separate tests of capability, authority, and consequence.

* Provisional Conclusion

The Tao3k modernization can be stated as unity without collapse. Epistemically, knowing, discriminating, justifying, acting, and verifying belong to one traceable, challengeable, and revisable chain of responsibility. Architecturally, claim, judgment, decision, authority, execution, and outcome must remain distinguishable states with distinguishable owners.

Philosophical unity therefore does not imply implementation collapse. Only by preserving these distinctions can we answer what the system knew, why it chose, who permitted action, what happened in the world, and how the next cycle must know differently.

* Questions That Remain

Before this becomes a shared principle, test three claims across both low- and high-consequence settings: how sound knowledge remains a proposal when authority is absent; how failure, partial success, and delayed consequence re-enter one update loop; and how people retain informed refusal and withdrawal at different stages. The responsibility chain must also prove that it supports challenge, correction, and stopping rather than merely producing more logs.

Also include cases where someone knows but presently lacks conditions for action. The system should help build conditions, identify institutional constraints or respect delay without rewriting scarce resources as weak will.
