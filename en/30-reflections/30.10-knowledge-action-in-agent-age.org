* Question
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: reflection-knowledge-action-in-agent-age-en
:END:

When an agent can read, reason, and invoke tools, how should knowledge and action form a loop without collapsing evidence, decision, and authority?

[[file:../40-sources/40.10-wang-yangming-knowledge-action.org][source note]] · [[file:../10-charter/10.40-action-and-authority.org][target thematic Charter]]

* Argument

Action must consume sourced knowledge, explicit judgment, and authorization; outcomes must return through receipts and observation. Closure is not a jump from model confidence to execution.

* Counterexamples

Read-only queries need not carry production-grade authorization; emergency stops may act before full explanation; some outcomes can only be observed later.

* Provisional Conclusion

The provisional conclusion is that knowledge and action constrain each other while proposal, decision, authority, execution, and outcome remain distinct.

* Promotion Criteria

Promote when authority boundaries, failure compensation, and real receipt patterns have executable evidence.
