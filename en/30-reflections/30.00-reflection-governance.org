* Question
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: reflection-question-en
:END:

Under what conditions may an illuminating idea become a cross-project constraint?

* Argument

Quotation and resonance do not confer normative authority. An idea must become
an explicit claim with counterarguments, failure modes, auditable engineering
consequences, and bounded scope. See the [[file:../10-charter/10.00-tao3k-charter.org][current Charter]].

* Counterexamples

“More automation is always better” converts easily into an implementation
slogan while failing to preserve consent, reversibility, or human agency.

* Provisional Conclusion

Reflection preserves dispute; Charter bears stable normative force. A file move
cannot transform one into the other.
