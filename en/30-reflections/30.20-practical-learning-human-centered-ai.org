* Question
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: reflection-practical-learning-human-centered-ai-en
:END:

How can technology answer to real human problems and consequences without reducing human value to an optimizable metric?

[[file:../40-sources/40.20-gu-yanwu-practical-learning.org][source note]] · [[file:../10-charter/10.70-practical-learning.org][target thematic Charter]]

* Argument

Engineering value must name beneficiaries, affected people, contestability, real effects, and long-term costs. Metrics are evidence, not value itself.

* Counterexamples

Infrastructure improvements may lack a direct user narrative; research may have no near-term deployment effect; stakeholder interests can conflict.

* Provisional Conclusion

The provisional requirement is that every high-impact optimization preserve human agency, stakeholder interests, and real-world receipts.

* Promotion Criteria

Promote when evaluation templates expose benefit, harm, unknowns, and challenge paths.
