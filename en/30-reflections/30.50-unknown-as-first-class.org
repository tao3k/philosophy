* Question
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: reflection-unknown-as-first-class-en
:END:

How should a system preserve unknown, conflicted, and hypothesized states rather than fill gaps with plausible completion?

[[file:../40-sources/40.30-scientific-method-evidence.org][source note]] · [[file:../10-charter/10.10-epistemology-and-uncertainty.org][target thematic Charter]]

* Argument

The unknown frontier determines what cannot be inferred, what retrieval or measurement is required, and whether action should pause. Losing unknown disguises missing evidence as fact.

* Counterexamples

Wrapping every value as unknown creates noise; low-risk defaults can suffice; engineering systems need computability rather than infinite doubt.

* Provisional Conclusion

Preserve unknown, conflicted, and hypothesized explicitly at decision-relevant boundaries, with provenance and resolution conditions.

* Promotion Criteria

Promote when negative tests prove that missing evidence cannot default to truth.
