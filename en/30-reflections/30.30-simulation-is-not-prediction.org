* Question
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: reflection-simulation-is-not-prediction-en
:END:

What claims can simulations, counterfactual trajectories, and bounded-horizon searches support, and what claims remain unsupported?

[[file:../40-sources/40.40-formal-methods-proof-counterexample.org][source note]] · [[file:../10-charter/10.20-temporality-and-causality.org][target thematic Charter]]

* Argument

A simulation output is a candidate trajectory under an explicit model, initial state, intervention, and horizon. It is neither observed fact nor automatically a probabilistic forecast or causal conclusion.

* Counterexamples

Calibrated predictive models can produce probabilities; digital twins can guide control; model checking can prove properties within a closed model.

* Provisional Conclusion

Every future-facing claim must distinguish observed, predicted, simulated, counterfactual, or unknown while retaining horizon and assumptions.
