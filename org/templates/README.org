* Further conversation within a document

Reflection, AI object-shift reflection and source-note templates include a local
=论道= / =Further Conversation= section. Its property drawer binds the subtree
contract =philosophy.dialogue-extension.v1= and gives it a stable =CUSTOM_ID=.
The document-level contract composition is unchanged. The section is not an
invitation to copy a central essay: develop the particular tension encountered
here, distinguish source and interpretation, and link to a relevant reading.

The contract requires prose, a reading link and an anchor within that subtree.
It cannot judge whether an objection is serious or the writing feels alive.
Keep the section at one level; adding child headings inherits subtree contracts
and therefore requires each child to satisfy the same checks.

New scaffolds require this extension. Existing documents acquire it when their
content is substantively revised; absence in an older document is not presented
as completed work, and no empty sections are added to manufacture coverage.

The currently pinned Pages builder (b11a7f97) does not route Org file search
suffixes such as =::#CUSTOM_ID=. Keep stable section anchors, but use ordinary
file links for the published reading paths until upstream supports that syntax.
Do not substitute generated, position-dependent HTML section IDs or claim
document-level links land directly on the extension.
